Consult a stronger advisor model for strategic guidance on hard decisions.

Use the advisor at high-leverage moments, where getting the plan right matters more than raw speed:

- Before committing to an approach on a large or ambiguous task (a refactor, a new design, a migration).
- When an error or test failure keeps recurring and your fixes are not converging.
- Before declaring a task complete, to independently check your work.

The advisor receives the full conversation so far — the task, every tool call, and its results — and returns concise, high-leverage guidance: a plan, a course correction, or a signal to stop. Apply the guidance, but if your own evidence contradicts a specific claim (a recommended step fails when you try it, or the files say otherwise), surface the conflict rather than following it blindly.

Reserve the advisor for genuine uncertainty. Do not call it every turn or for routine steps you can already handle — each call consults a larger model and costs time and tokens. In `question`, state clearly what you are stuck on or the decision you want reviewed, including the tradeoffs, the error, or the plan you are weighing.
